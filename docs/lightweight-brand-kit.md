# Rententio Lightweight Brand Kit

## Brand Summary
- Rententio is an AI‑powered video editor focused on improving viewer retention.
- It segments clips, scores retention, and reorders content to maximize watch‑time.

## Audience
- Creators who value speed, clarity, and results.

## Positioning
- Retention‑first editing with minimal manual effort.
- Built for creators, not for complex pro editing workflows.

## Tone of Voice
- Confident, calm, and data‑driven.
- Clear and direct; avoid hype or buzzwords.

## Typography
### Font Stack
- Primary: Playfair Display (400, 600, 700) for headlines and hero text
- Secondary: Inter (300, 400, 500, 600) for UI and body
- Accent (optional): Cormorant Garamond (400, 400 Italic, 600) for short emphasis only

### Hierarchy Scale
- Hero: 64–96px (Playfair Display Bold)
- H1: 48–56px (Playfair Display SemiBold)
- H2: 36–40px (Playfair Display SemiBold)
- H3: 28–32px (Inter SemiBold)
- H4: 20–24px (Inter Medium)
- Body Large: 18–20px (Inter Regular)
- Body Default: 16px (Inter Regular)
- Body Small: 14px (Inter Regular)
- Caption/Legal: 12–13px (Inter Light)

### Line Height
- Playfair headlines: 1.1–1.2
- Inter body: 1.6–1.75
- Captions: 1.5

### Letter Spacing
- Playfair headlines: -0.02em to -0.01em
- Inter body: 0
- Inter ALL CAPS (buttons/labels): +0.05em to +0.1em
- Gold text: +0.02em

### Italics Usage
Use:
- Pull quotes: Cormorant Garamond Italic, 24–28px
- Light emphasis: Inter Italic, sparingly
- Testimonial attribution: Inter Light Italic, 14px
- Decorative subheadings: Playfair Display Italic, short only
Avoid:
- Primary headlines
- Buttons/CTAs
- Navigation
- Long body paragraphs

### Font Pairing Rules
- Hero headline: Playfair Display Bold, White or Luxury Gold, 64–96px
- Section headers: Playfair Display SemiBold, White, 36–48px
- Subheadings: Inter SemiBold, Muted Gold or White, 20–24px, +0.05em tracking
- Body text: Inter Regular, Soft White or Text Secondary, 16–18px, 1.6–1.75 line height
- Buttons/CTAs: Inter SemiBold, White on Gold BG, 14–16px, ALL CAPS, +0.1em tracking
- Captions: Inter Light, Text Secondary, 12–14px
- Pull quotes: Cormorant Garamond Italic, Luxury Gold, 24–28px

### UI-Specific Best Practices
- Navigation: Inter Medium, 14–16px, +0.05em tracking; hover uses Luxury Gold
- Buttons: Inter SemiBold, 14–16px, ALL CAPS; never italic
- Forms: labels Inter Medium 14px; input Inter Regular 16px; placeholders Inter Light 300
- Cards: titles Playfair Display SemiBold 24–28px; descriptions Inter Regular 16px; metadata Inter Light 12–14px

### Contrast Pairing
- Light backgrounds: Rich Black text + Dark Gold accents
- Dark backgrounds: Soft White text + Luxury Gold accents
- Avoid pure white on pure black; prefer Soft White on Rich Black
- Gold text legibility: minimum 18px and use for short text only

## Color System (Black, White & Gold)
### Core Palette
- Rich Black: #0A0A0A (primary backgrounds, text on light surfaces)
- Pure White: #FFFFFF (primary text on dark, clean backgrounds)
- Luxury Gold: #D4AF37 (primary accents, CTAs, highlights)

### Extended Palette
- Charcoal: #1A1A1A (secondary backgrounds, elevated surfaces)
- Soft White: #F8F8F8 (light backgrounds, cards)
- Muted Gold: #B8941E (hover states, secondary accents)
- Pale Gold: #F4E7C3 (subtle highlights, borders)
- Dark Gold: #8B7028 (deep accents, shadows)

### Functional Colors
- Divider (Dark): #2A2A2A (borders on dark backgrounds)
- Divider (Light): #E5E5E5 (borders on light backgrounds)
- Text Secondary (Dark): #A0A0A0 (muted text on dark)
- Text Secondary (Light): #666666 (muted text on light)

### Gradients
- Gold Shimmer: linear-gradient(135deg, #D4AF37 0%, #F4E7C3 50%, #D4AF37 100%)
- Dark Depth: linear-gradient(180deg, #0A0A0A 0%, #1A1A1A 100%)
- Light Glow: linear-gradient(180deg, #FFFFFF 0%, #F8F8F8 100%)

### Accessibility Notes
- White on Rich Black: WCAG AAA (21:1 contrast)
- Luxury Gold on Rich Black: WCAG AA (8.2:1 contrast)
- Rich Black on Soft White: WCAG AAA (19.8:1 contrast)
- Avoid Luxury Gold text on white backgrounds; use Dark Gold (#8B7028) instead

## UX Guardrails
- One primary action per screen.
- Manual override is always visible.
- Progress and status are always visible.
- Data explains decisions without overwhelming the user.

## AI Presence
- Helpful and transparent, not mysterious or intimidating.
- Narrate what AI is doing in simple language.
