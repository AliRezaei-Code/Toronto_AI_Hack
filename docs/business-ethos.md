# Rententio Business Ethos

## Purpose
- Improve viewer retention through fast, intelligent editing.
- Reduce manual effort while keeping creators in control.

## Product Philosophy
- Clarity over complexity.
- Speed to value over excessive options.
- Data guides decisions, it does not overwhelm.

## Role of AI
- AI is a helpful co‑editor, not a black box.
- AI actions are visible and explained in plain language.
- Users can always override AI decisions.

## User Priorities
- Fast results and minimal friction.
- Clear signals about what to do next.
- Confidence that the system is working.

## Experience Principles
- One primary action per screen.
- Progress is always visible.
- Retention insights are simple and actionable.
